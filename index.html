Questions

Is PyCep free, and do I need an account?

It is free and there is no account to create. Open it and start typing.

Where is my code saved?

On your own device, inside the app or browser. Nothing is uploaded, so clearing your browser data also clears your files.

Which Python does it run?

Python 3, running inside your browser. The core language and common modules such as math and random work. Large libraries like NumPy and pandas are not available.

What do I get when I download it?

On Windows and Mac you get a small launcher that opens PyCep in its own window, without tabs or an address bar. On iPad, iPhone and Android, PyCep is added to your home screen. The editor is the same everywhere and loads from the web, so you need an internet connection the first time.
